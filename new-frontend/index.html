“Gold is not merely a precious metal in our atelier; it is a sacred parchment upon which dynasties record their immortality.”

Founded in 1914 in the royal princely states of India, Shree Rani Gehna was appointed as the privileged purveyor of ceremonial jewels to the maharanis and royal households. For five generations, our master goldsmiths have preserved secret metallurgical alloys, pure 24K Jadau burnishing, and unhurried hand-hammering techniques that modern machines cannot emulate.

Every high-jewelry creation from the Masterpiece Archive requires between four to twelve months of single-artisan dedication, resulting in pieces of unmatched museum-grade significance.

Anatomy of an Incomparable
Royal Heirloom

A harmonious symphony of four centuries-old jewelry crafts unified in a singular masterpiece.

01

Nakshi Temple Repoussé

Crafted from a single solid sheet of 22K gold, embossed in reverse on tree resin pitch, then intricately carved from the front with customized micro-chisels to achieve 1.8mm sculptural relief.

Time: 420+ Hours Tools: Chased Steel Gravers
02

Pure Jadau Kundan Setting

Uncut polki diamonds are seated with hyper-refined 24K pure gold foils (kundan) burnished cold under intense pressure, creating a seamless, hermetically sealed gem setting with no solder.

Foil: 24K Hyper-Pure Luster: Antique Moonlight
03

Royal Meenakari Enameling

The reverse of the necklace is enveloped in vibrant vitreous mineral enamel fired at 850°C. Mineral cobalt, copper green, and ruby red form an intimate secret garden resting against the skin.

Firing: 850°C Kiln Finish: Vitreous Porcelain
04

Pori & Basra Pearl Stringing

Natural antique Basra seed pearls and certified Zambian emerald beads are individually knotted with unbleached raw silk strands, weighted perfectly for royal ergonomic balance.

Thread: Pure Silk Cord Pearls: Natural Basra
